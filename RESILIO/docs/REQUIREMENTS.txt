**Assigned → In Progress → Awaiting Confirmation → Delivered**

Requirements:

- Prevent reservations exceeding available stock or unallocated need.
- Prevent duplicate delivery confirmation.
- Support partial delivery.
- Release unused reserved quantities after partial completion.
- Release reservations when an assignment is cancelled.
- Mark a request fulfilled only when confirmed deliveries satisfy its quantity.
