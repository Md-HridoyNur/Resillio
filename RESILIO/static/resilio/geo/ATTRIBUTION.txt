Original boundary license: Creative Commons CC0.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
ADM2: 64 districts. Boundary reference year 2020. Sources Bangladesh Bureau of
Statistics (BBS) / OCHA Regional Office for Asia and the Pacific (ROAP).
Original license: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/

Adaptations for RESILIO: simplified source geometry, coordinate precision reduced
to five decimals, consistent display names and parent division codes added.
Representative points are approximate district summaries, not actual facilities.
The boundaries are illustrative; they are not navigation or survey measurements.

geoBoundaries database attribution: Runfola et al. (2020), geoBoundaries:
A global database of political administrative boundaries. PLoS ONE 15(4):e0231866.
